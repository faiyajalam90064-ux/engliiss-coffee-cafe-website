About Engliiss Coffee Cafe

Engliiss Coffee Cafe is a premium yet highly affordable coffee destination located right inside Shop No. 6 at Bundelkhand University, Jhansi.

Whether you need an early morning energy boost, a quiet spot to catch up on studies, or a cozy corner to chill with friends, Engliiss Coffee Cafe brings you richly brewed espresso, soothing teas, refreshing cold beverages, and freshly made snacks—all priced thoughtfully for students, campus visitors, and local residents.
